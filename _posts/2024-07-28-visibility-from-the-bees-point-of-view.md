Meeting with Kush and Noora to show the choreographed trajectories implemented for the dandelion.

Noora felt the pollen wasn't visible enough: its path didn't fill enough of the bee's field of view, which hurt both gameplay and the enjoyment of the choreography.

We set an ideal height for the pollen, looked at movement type A3, and sketched how trajectories should scale in proportion to the flower.
